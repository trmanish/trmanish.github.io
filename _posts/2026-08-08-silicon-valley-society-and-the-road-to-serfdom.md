**Energy:**

One of the most used yet, misunderstood theme of our lives is Energy.

Our entire life, starting from waking up to sleeping runs on energy. Yet, somehow, most ppl never tried learning how exactly Energy Systems work around the world.

As a human species, our sources of Energy have slowly seen a transition to different sources of fuel.

- ***Mechanical Energy of Muscles- the only source of Energy***

Thousands of years ago, The Cave-person, had no access to **external energy** at their disposal. We were no different than animals, except that we weren't as strong as them. But just like an animal, our energy was limited to the access to our internal energy, ***the mechanical energy of muscles***which we would have after converting it from Chemical Energy of Foods we would eat.

If you didn't have food one day, we wouldn't be able to do any work much, which was anyway largely involved in hunting and searching for food to survive, because there isn't much conversion of Chemical Energy of food to Mechanical Energy in Body. That's all we could do back then.

Then as time progressed, we as a human species, ***discovered Fire***. This was one of the major "invention" of our entire human history. Fire not only provided an external source of Energy, which would help us now cook raw food and even potentially scare away animals, it led to the development of ***Language for our species***.

Fire, led to gradual shrinkage of stomach and intestines in our body, since we no longer had to eat raw food, and food can be cooked. This with time, led to development of bigger brain and eventually Language.

Language helped us strategize, plan and co-ordinate amongst us better than other animal species, thereby making us overcome the strength of animals and helped us capture and domesticate many of the animals. We as a species, slowly now started rising up the Food Chain as we can now fight the bigger animals like Tiger etc and domesticate other stronger animals like horses, cows.

- This led to further addition in our Energy Source- ***Animal Energy***

The horses, oxen, cows, bulls and elephants led to our increased mobility across long distances. We started moving and traveling far off distances, helped spread the species to far off land and started inter-regional trade amongst us.

The human species was no longer relegated to searching and hunting for food in a small radius. We could transport and seek food from other places where it could be grown favorably compared to the place one lived. Trade started to flourish because we could now harness more energy at our disposal- the external energy provided by horses, elephants.

We also leveraged oxen and bulls, to now help us irrigate land, to grow food, instead of hunting for food.

Until 1800, our primary source of **Energy for heating was largely wood, a biomass**. For other kinetic energy, we relied on muscle energy from animals or our own.

<img src="/assets/sv_serfdom/img_14.webp" width="100%">

- **From Biomass, to Coal**

Sometime in mid to late 1800s, our transition from Wood to Coal started happening. We invented Steam Engine, running on Coal and our mobility, transport of goods/food further increased. We had more Energy at our disposal now.

- **Coal to Oil and Natural Gas**

Post mid 1900's we started increasing our reliance on Oil and natural gas (a bye product of drilling oil). Advantage of Oil was that it was in liquid form, so quite easy to use in various applications especially for Jet fuel where Coal could not efficiently help power the engine for jets due to its solid nature of the fuel.

<img src="/assets/sv_serfdom/img_15.webp" width="100%">

Oil also brought itself with other refined products like Gasoline, Kerosene for heating, Naptha and Petroleum based products that are used almost in every product we use in life. Petroleum led to creation of polymers and plastics that drives medical equipments/devices.

<img src="/assets/sv_serfdom/img_16.webp" width="100%">

As you see, since thousands of years, our species has slowly moved from an energy source that used to be [**low on Energy Density to a higher Energy Density fuel**](https://www.engineeringtoolbox.com/fossil-fuels-energy-content-d_1298.html). That has propelled us to have more energy at our disposal in the last 50 years than anytime ever in our history.

**More than 100 years ago, 70% of the population was engaged in growing food**. That was due to manual labor of planting crops, harvesting, transportation. Because we had limited supply of external source of energy.

This allowed us to focus our time and effort as a species in advancing science, technology, better healthcare solutions, better industrial systems to manage natural calamities and disasters, work on software technology to help make our lives easier and efficient.

Everything you enjoy, benefit from science and technological advancement, the internet, the smartphones, everything is possible because we as a society got the luxury of more energy at our disposal to allow us to grow food at a far more efficient way, allowing a large section of population to focus their efforts on other areas.

**And the only reason we have been able to do this is because of fossil fuels.**

Instead of using 30 oxen in 1800 to using tens of horses in 1900 ploughing the land and growing food, we now use machines built by fossil fuels, driven by fossil fuels to grow, harvest, dry, store and transport food in as efficient manner as it can.

**The most important element on our planet is Ammonia**. You can literally get rid of semi conductors, steel, cement from the planet and yet it wont threaten the survival of our species.

But you cannot eliminate Ammonia.

Ammonia provides the Nitrogen needed to produce fertilizers.

You remove ammonia , 50% of the world will die immediately as there won’t be enough food to feed a population of 7.9B ppl.

This Ammonia comes from Natural Gas, a fossil fuel via a process called [Haber's Process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haber_process), one of the most important Invention of our humanity that won Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Without Haber's Process, our civilization would not have survived as there wasn't enough nitrogen available for crops to grow from natural fertilizer. Without crops nourishment, yield of food was plunging in early decades of 20th century and the world was on the brink of dying.

Haber Bosch Process was successfully able to synthesize Ammonia at Industrial Scale from Natural Gas, to provide mass scale nitrogen to be used as fertilizer for a growing population.

This was only possible due to access to Natural Gas, a fossil fuel we have been told is evil for us.
